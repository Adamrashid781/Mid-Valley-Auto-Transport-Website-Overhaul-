Our Story

Built on Trust
Driven by Service

Mid Valley Auto Transport Inc. was founded in 2014 by Rashid Rashid who recognized a critical gap in the auto transport industry: the need for true honesty, accountability, and integrity.

Drawing from extensive experience in the field, Rashid built the company on these core principles — values that continue to define everything we do.

From individual vehicle shipments to large-scale logistics, every client is treated with equal respect, attention, and professionalism.

We believe every customer deserves a transparent, stress-free experience, regardless of the size or complexity of the job.

With years of hands-on experience in vehicle logistics, our team understands the importance of precision and reliability. We’ve developed a nationwide network of licensed, insured carriers who share our commitment to safety, efficiency, and excellence.
